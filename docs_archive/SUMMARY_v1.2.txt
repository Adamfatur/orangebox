6. SORTING SEQUENCE (5 PHASES)
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

FASE 1 - SELECTOR POSITIONING (0.3s)
  → Selector moves to BIN A (60°) or BIN B (120°)

FASE 2 - UNLOCK & DROP (0.1s)
  → Both locks unlock PARALLEL (90° → 0°)
  → Container drops by GRAVITY

FASE 3 - WAITING (1.0s)
  → Wait for waste to fall and slide

FASE 4 - LIFT & LOCK (0.4s)
  → All 4 corners lift PARALLEL (90° → 0°)
  → Both locks lock PARALLEL (0° → 90°)

FASE 5 - RESET (0.3s)
  → Selector returns to NEUTRAL (90°)

TOTAL TIME: ~2-3 seconds per cycle
